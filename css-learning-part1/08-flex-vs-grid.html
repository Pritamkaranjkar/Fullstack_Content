Decision Rule

Use Flexbox when you mainly want to arrange items in a single row or a single column.

Use Grid when you need to arrange items in both rows and columns.

Real-Life Example: Shopping Website

Imagine a shopping website. The navigation bar might look like:

Logo | Home | Products | About | Contact

These items are mainly arranged in one row, so Flexbox is a good choice.

The products might be displayed like this: 

Product 1    Product 2    Product 3
Product 4    Product 5    Product 6

Here we have both rows and columns, so Grid is a good choice.

Easy rule to remember: Flexbox → row or column. Grid → rows and columns.

Do not choose Grid just because it is newer, and do not use Flexbox for every layout. Choose the layout system that best matches the design you want to create.
